# Penetration-Test Report QA Checklist

## Technical Accuracy

- [ ] Every finding was reproduced or explicitly marked unverified.
- [ ] Targets, endpoints, parameters, roles, dates, and versions are correct.
- [ ] Evidence supports each technical claim.
- [ ] Preconditions and limitations are stated.
- [ ] Observed impact is separated from potential impact.
- [ ] Scanner output was manually validated.
- [ ] Reproduction steps use the least destructive proof.

## Risk and Remediation

- [ ] The approved risk model was used consistently.
- [ ] Scoring version, vector, and rationale are recorded when applicable.
- [ ] Findings are rated independently.
- [ ] Attack chains are explained separately.
- [ ] Remediation fixes the root cause.
- [ ] Defense-in-depth controls are not presented as substitutes.
- [ ] Retest criteria are measurable.

## Scope and Coverage

- [ ] Scope matches the authorization and Rules of Engagement.
- [ ] Approved scope changes are documented.
- [ ] Tested, partial, untested, and out-of-scope assets are distinguished.
- [ ] Constraints and gaps are visible in the main report or appendix.
- [ ] No conclusion implies more assurance than the coverage supports.

## Security and Privacy

- [ ] Passwords, tokens, cookies, keys, and secrets are masked.
- [ ] Personal and regulated data are minimized and redacted.
- [ ] Metadata, filenames, screenshots, and comments were checked for leakage.
- [ ] Evidence storage and report delivery follow the agreed controls.
- [ ] Remaining artifacts and cleanup owners are listed.

## Editorial Quality

- [ ] The executive summary was written after the findings.
- [ ] Language is objective, direct, and professional.
- [ ] Completed testing is described in past tense.
- [ ] First-person and informal language were removed.
- [ ] Terminology, spelling, headings, identifiers, and severity labels are consistent.
- [ ] Acronyms are expanded on first use.
- [ ] Tables, links, references, and cross-references work.
- [ ] A peer reviewer completed an independent pass.

## Delivery

- [ ] Version, date, classification, and recipients are correct.
- [ ] The final file opens and renders correctly.
- [ ] Only approved recipients have access.
- [ ] Critical findings were already escalated as required.
- [ ] The delivery and retention process is recorded.
- [ ] Retest expectations and next actions are clear.
